# Surface brief: ClearBreath single page

Scope: whole page (web). Visitor mode: Operate with a Persuade first screen. Two selectable page shapes ("Showcase" hero-first, "Tool" tool-first) are built and compared live; the user picks one.

Audience, job, action: Indian families in smog season; read the air now, build a household plan, ask a follow-up. Proof is live Open-Meteo data and the exposure model; no invented stats.

Unresolved: which page shape ships (the user decides from the in-page tabs).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a room of air. A canvas of drifting haze has the density of today's real AQI, headline letters begin as blurred smog and resolve to sharp type, and building a plan visibly thins the haze by the estimated exposure cut. Refuses the dashboard-of-cards arrangement.

OWN-WORLD: Smog-dusk ground (warm brown-grey, oklch L about 0.18), pale warm ink, amber for sun-through-smog and pollution, one cool clear-air teal that only marks what is clean or improved. Gloock display serif over Hanken Grotesk body. Panels are lightness steps, not glass or cards-in-cards. AQI badge uses its six CPCB category colours as data tokens. Recognizable with all content removed by the haze canvas and the amber/teal pair.

STORY: Visitor sees today's real AQI in their city dissolve into focus, understands the air is bad and what that means for a family, tells the page about their home, watches the haze thin, and leaves with three top actions and the reasons.

FIRST VIEWPORT (Showcase): full-height haze canvas; left-aligned giant headline resolving from blur; one sentence; primary action "Build my plan"; live city readout (city select, AQI number, category word) large at right-bottom. Tool shape: compact resolving headline, AQI strip, form and results side by side from the first screen.

FORM: Canvas-first atmospheric tool, from the user's pick "Living haze", with a React fluid-text and canvas-haze signature. seed: none (pinned): "Living haze".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
